1 · Collect a creator's Reels

recommended

Works for any public creator, free. It runs in your own browser and only reads what's on the page you're viewing (never your login or cookies), including each Reel's views, likes and comments.

  1. Drag 📥 Collect Reels to your bookmarks bar (once; re-drag after updates).
  2. Open the creator's Reels tab on instagram.com.
  3. Click the bookmark, then Auto-scroll until it says it reached the end.
  4. Press Import. ReelScript opens with every Reel filled in on the right — press Start import.
